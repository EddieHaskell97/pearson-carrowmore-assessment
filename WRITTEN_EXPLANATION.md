# Written explanation

## Scope and decision policy

I answered one bounded question using the entirely synthetic Pearson assessment pack: which current keepers in the permitted 40-profile subset plausibly merit an Array O&M interview, and what would each need first? Thirty-eight profiles are current; K1203 and K1458 are retained for traceability but remain outside the redeployment cohort. This is not a ranking of the full population.

The final result is 36 supported technical interviews and two targeted screens. These are observed outcomes, not quotas. A supported result requires specific, attributed evidence of plausible hands-on mechanical **or** electrical maintenance, with the activity and its transferable relevance stated. Targeted screening is reserved for a material unresolved attribution problem or contradiction about that core claim. Missing, sparse, repetitive or old records, supervision comments and adverse appraisals are not automatic gates. Insufficient evidence would mean the bounded sources do not establish the essential experience; it would not mean that the person lacks it. Counts, dates and canonical workflows remain descriptive only. No numerical threshold, recency window, confidence score or keyword veto decides a disposition.

Every current profile was reviewed individually. Positive and adverse manager testimony remains together, and year-only 2026 appraisals remain time-uncertain. K1345 is supported on cited electrical and mechanical work while AP003873 and AP003877 remain interview questions alongside AP003865, AP003870 and AP003874. K0566 is supported by CL010043 and CL006319 despite only four canonical workflows. K0310 is supported on historical cited work, but the logs end in 2012 and no keyed appraisal is supplied, so subsequent practice must be explored. K1200 requires targeted screening because CL005994 and the other attributed hands-on records materially conflict with AP003496. K0478 is the other targeted case because the electrical/isolation logs materially conflict with AP001159, AP001160, AP001162 and AP001165. Neither source is silently preferred.

## Traceability and requirements

The evaluator reads the source ZIP directly, enforces its hashes and the explicit 2026-10-11 scenario date, and emits JSON plus readable Markdown. Every claimed category has an exact displayed work citation. Original events, dates, quotes and references are preserved while superficial measurement and `permit` / `PTW` / `permit bk.` variants collapse only in descriptive workflow counts. Suspect measurement units are flagged without rewriting the quotation. Null-key appraisals are not guessed onto people; clean/manual attribution and raw corroboration remain distinct.

The O&M posting requires mechanical **or** electrical maintenance. Requirements for the separate HV Authorised Person appointment are not imported as O&M interview gates, and the insurer-note boundary remains unresolved. The OSSC/WAHS mapping is explicitly provisional where employer acceptance and climbing/rescue scope are unspecified. Permit, isolation and practical checks are recommendations unless a supplied posting expressly requires them. Fictional scheme periods and prerequisites are preserved; external GWO rules are not substituted. Nominal expiry calculations are consistency checks, and issuer verification is required where supplied and calculated dates conflict. `Pass`, `Attended`, `Not achieved` and missing evidence remain distinct. Interview support is separate from application eligibility, assessed competence, authorisation and mobilisation readiness.

The independent QA harness reads the archive and generated files without importing evaluator logic. It checks exact source text and citations, individual policy fields, time handling, requirement provenance, action dependencies and UTF-8 rendering. Passing engineering checks establishes reproducibility and fidelity, not predictive validity or recruitment correctness. The original Pearson brief is not currently present in this workspace; its recorded hash and the findings from the copy inspected during the rules audit are retained rather than invented.

## Deliberate limits and effort

I did not build a UI, service, database, ML model, full-population resolver or deployment. Current medical fitness, employer acceptance, willingness, competence and authorisation remain unestablished. Certificate verification precedes conditional renewal, assessment or training actions; the employer's stated funding applies to renewals, not an invented first-time route.
