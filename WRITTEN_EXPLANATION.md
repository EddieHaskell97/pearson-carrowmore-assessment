# Written explanation

## What I deliberately did not build, and why

I used the permitted 40-profile subset to answer a narrower, useful question: which current keepers have evidence supporting an Array O&M interview, and what must happen before they could progress? That left 38 current people and two leavers. I did not build a UI, service, deployment, candidate ranking or full-population identity resolver. The assessment's value is in making claims answerable and traceable, not adding infrastructure. The separate personal ML/SQL lab was not part of this implementation.

The posting accepts mechanical **or** electrical maintenance experience and offers training. I therefore separated interview potential from verified qualifications, competence and mobilisation. The result is 36 supported technical interviews and two targeted screens, without a quota. K0478 and K1200 have material contradictions about their core work claims, so clarification comes first. Other adverse comments remain interview questions rather than automatic vetoes. Existing certificate evidence should be verified before missing coverage becomes a training prescription.

Using the clean subset was a cut in scope, not permission to trust its summaries. I compared material claims with detailed records and preserved disagreements, including expired module evidence behind valid-looking summaries and a future completion behind a year-only claim. I left unresolved identity joins unresolved instead of manufacturing matches. With the full raw population, I would first need a reliable identity-resolution process and a broader evidence review.

## Where AI was wrong, and where I took control

An early answer recommended interviewing every current keeper while omitting the main appraisal file. I challenged that result and required appraisal evidence to be reconciled with the work logs. The next approach overcorrected by inventing pattern-count thresholds and a ten-year adverse-appraisal gate. I asked where those rules came from. They were not supplied requirements, so we removed them and returned to person-specific evidence.

I also required another source-to-output review rather than accepting repeated assurances that the package was finished. That exposed missing trade wording, inflated template diversity, omitted station notes, future training leaking through clean summaries and chronology conflicts. Corrections retained original quotations and competing claims rather than quietly rewriting the data to make the answer tidy. Positive manager testimony stayed positive, even when a profile also contained concerns.

AI assisted the implementation and bounded source review. All 305 exactly keyed appraisal rows were mechanically processed; 180 technical-topic candidates were selected through an AI-assisted source review. That is not a claim that I personally read every sentence in the pack. I retained independent QA because wrong-person joins, wrong dates and inaccurate citations would undermine the central answer. Its passing checks support source fidelity and repeatability, not proof that a recruitment judgment is correct.

## What I would replace and retain in a real product

I would replace vocabulary-only skill interpretation and hardcoded person-specific judgments as the scope expanded. They make this small synthetic exercise inspectable, but a real product needs agreed employer criteria, representative evaluation and a review process for ambiguous evidence. I would keep useful deterministic parsing and normalization rather than discarding them merely because some interpretation currently uses keywords.

I would also keep exact citations, original quotations, source fingerprints, explicit scenario dates and visible provenance. Pass, Attended and Not achieved must remain distinct. An interview lead must stay separate from a verified credential, assessed competence and current authorisation. Missing evidence should trigger verification, not an accusation. Those distinctions make the result useful without hiding its limits.

I spent **three and a half hours on the assessment-specific work and review**, excluding a separate **30-minute personal ML/SQL learning exercise**. Recorded agent execution intervals are separate from that effort statement.
