## 1. What was deliberately not built, and why

This review draft answers one bounded question using the synthetic Pearson pack: which of the 38 current keepers in the permitted 40-profile subset has specific, attributed evidence supporting a plausible Array O&M technical interview, and what must be checked next? K1203 and K1458 remain outside the cohort. I did not build a UI, service, database, deployment, new model, full-population resolver or candidate ranking. The final outcome is 36 supported interviews and two targeted screens, with no quota or numerical gate. Supported means specific hands-on mechanical **or** electrical evidence warrants a structured interview. Targeted screening is reserved for a material unresolved contradiction about the core claim: K1200's attributed work versus AP003496, and K0478's electrical/isolation history versus mixed switching testimony. K0478's positive 2017 station note and AP001161 are retained without treating them as authorisation or proof that later concerns were resolved. Missing, sparse or old records and supervision comments do not establish a lack of experience.

They are transparent enough for this synthetic, bounded exercise but cannot establish predictive validity, resolve identity disputes or support operational employment decisions. A real product would require an agreed human policy, lawful representative evaluation, access and correction routes, and qualified employer/issuer review.
